#4471 · reassessing date reports

owner support desk reviewed reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

The original incident page treats the merged date reports as one problem. The export describes two different problems, but its automatic classification does not establish the cause of every report. This page describes the 4 October 2026 export, not the current queue.

Two causes

  • Shared parser: it reads dates month-first even for tenants who write the day first. Ambiguous dates can be interpreted as the wrong day.
  • booking-v3 hint: the form displays MM/DD/YYYY to day-first tenants. People follow the hint, but the form interprets the entry in the tenant’s order.

The table deduplicates by report ID. For reports not flagged by the desk, it uses the existing classification based on tenant locale, form and channel. That classification does not read report bodies, so its counts are candidates, not confirmed root causes.

The desk flagged five reports for a different reading. They are set aside for review, not treated as five confirmed cases of one new bug:

  • R-41202: an invite for 12 May appeared in the calendar on 11 May.
  • R-41209: bookings in June were reported one day off.
  • R-41236: an invite arrived before the booking was made; no one-day shift is stated.
  • R-41344: people in Lisbon and Toronto received different days for the same meeting. Time zones may explain this, but the report does not establish the cause or a defect.
  • R-41620: Tuesday 6 October appeared as Monday.

Reported engineering update

An engineering update relayed to us says #4471 closed on 5 October, the parser was fixed with fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales, and the hint will not be fixed because booking-v3 was retired. These are reported outcomes; this page does not independently verify a deployment or the retirement.
